Print a new center label, and bring it to life

Circle labels
A replacement center label and its DJ strip, together on one ordinary 4×6 shipping label. Cut out the disc, stick it over a worn, blank or mislabeled center, and slip the strip on the sleeve. Three sizes: 7" with the large jukebox hole, 7" with the small spindle hole, and 12", which lists the tracks you tick. Pick the side and the speed, and a cue mark at the top lines up with the first beat.
Zoetropes
A circle label can carry a ring of animation frames that comes alive when the record spins. Watch it through your phone’s camera, or long‑press LIGHT to strobe the flashlight at it. Make one from your own Live Photo: frame it, trim the clip, and set how bold it prints.
Munbyn FM226
A second Bluetooth printer brand. The FM226 takes the same 50mm die‑cut labels as the Phomemo M110 and prints the same DJ labels, seller tags and stickers, on battery, with no network and no outlet.
A Label Designer that explains itself
A card at the top says your design now replaces the built‑in label on that stock, and shows you the one it replaces. Tap a field to add it, undo and redo anything, and set weight, all caps and kerning. One switch on the print screen flips a single label between the default and your design.
  • Collection rows have a button for everything they do: add to your active crate, mark as labeled, add a copy, remove (hold to confirm), and print.
  • Discogs collection folders: see, create and rename them, and choose which one new records go into.
  • YouTube tracks can have their tempo and key set by hand, right under the playing video, and the tracklist shows each track’s tempo.
  • Built‑in seller tags print the sleeve grade beside the media grade.
  • Vibes come in six groups (Energy, Dancefloor, Lead, Set time, Keeper, Reference), plus a clean or explicit mark of its own.
  • Close the player and you’re back where you were in your collection.
  • Every print sheet opens on the last label you printed, and marks the sizes your Bluetooth printer takes.
  • A label that came out wrong can be reported from the Bluetooth Printer screen, with what the printer said attached.
  • Switched a track to YouTube by mistake? Undo it for a few seconds after. A track on neither Apple Music nor YouTube links to TunesToTube, so you can upload it for everyone.
  • Tap LIGHT for a steady light that stays on until you tap it again.
  • Large text sizes no longer make the app overlap itself.
  • One‑time tips for six gestures nobody was told about, like swiping up on the cover for the pitch fader.
